POOL FUNDING

Pool Funding allows multiple investors to combine funds together to leverage larger trading opportunities and increase overall earning potential.

📖 How It Works

Investors contribute to a shared trading pool managed by JMJ. Profits generated are distributed evenly based on the agreed structure of the funding pool.

✨ What You Gain

  • Access to larger trading opportunities
  • Increased scalability
  • Shared risk exposure
  • Passive income opportunities
  • Professional fund management

SIGNALS

Signals allows traders to copy JMJ's technical analysis and trading ideas directly onto their own trading accounts while keeping 100% of the profits they make.

📖 How It Works

JMJ provides entry points, stop losses, take profits, and market direction based on technical analysis. Traders simply follow and execute the trades on their personal accounts.

✨ What You Gain

  • Access to professional analysis
  • Better trade opportunities
  • Improved market understanding
  • Full control of your capital
  • Keep all profits made from your trades

CLASSES

📖 How It Works

Students can learn either in a group class or through private mentorship for faster and more personalized learning.

✨ What You Gain

  • Deep trading knowledge
  • Real market experience
  • Personal trading confidence
  • Access to proven strategies
  • The ability to trade independently

PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT
