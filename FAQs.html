FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I start with JMJ?

▼

Simply create an account, choose your preferred service (Pool Funding, Signals, Account Management, or Mentorship), and complete the verification process. Most users are fully set up within 24 hours.

What's the minimum deposit?

▼

For Pool Funding, you can start with as little as $250. Signals and Mentorship have different entry points starting from $50/month. Account Management typically requires a minimum of $3,000.

Can I withdraw my funds anytime?

▼

Yes. Pool Funding has monthly liquidity windows, while personal accounts under Signals and Mentorship allow withdrawals anytime. Account Management has a 30-day notice period for larger withdrawals.

Which broker do you use?

▼

We trade exclusively on Deriv, a regulated broker specializing in synthetic indices. All JMJ services are built around Deriv's instruments including Volatility indices, Step index, and Jump indices.

Is JMJ available in my country?

▼

JMJ services are available globally, except in countries where forex trading is restricted. Check our terms page for the full list of restricted jurisdictions or contact support.

How is JMJ different from other signal providers?

▼

We offer multiple ways to participate, not just signals. Pool Funding, Account Management, and Mentorship provide flexibility. Our 92% accuracy is verified and our team consists of active traders, not just analysts.

What support do I get after joining?

▼

All members get access to our private community, 24/7 chat support, and regular market updates. Mentorship students receive 1-on-1 sessions, while Account Management clients get monthly performance reviews.

What if i have more questions?

▼

You can reach our support team via Email. Contact us on via Live chat or Telegram for faster response.
