Risk Disclosure

Last Updated: March 17, 2026

⚠️ IMPORTANT: READ CAREFULLY

Trading forex and synthetic indices carries a HIGH LEVEL OF RISK and may not be suitable for all investors. Before deciding to participate in the financial markets, you should carefully consider your investment objectives, level of experience, and risk appetite.

1. No Guarantee of Profit

JMJ Trading provides signals, account management, pool funding, and mentorship services based on our experience and analysis. However, past performance does not guarantee future results. There is no guarantee that you will make profits, and you may lose some or all of your invested capital.

2. High-Risk Nature of Trading

Trading forex, synthetic indices (including Volatility 75, Boom & Crash, Step Indices), and other financial derivatives involves significant risk. The leveraged nature of these products means that small market movements can have a substantial impact on your account. You could lose your entire investment quickly.

3. Synthetic Indices Specific Risks

Synthetic indices offered by brokers like Deriv are computer-generated markets that simulate real-market conditions. While they are not affected by real-world economic events, they have their own unique characteristics and risks:

  • Volatility Risk: Volatility indices can experience rapid and unpredictable price movements
  • Boom & Crash Risk: These indices have sudden spikes that can trigger stop-losses unexpectedly
  • Step Index Risk: Step indices move in distinct steps and can reverse direction quickly
  • Leverage Risk: The leverage available can amplify both profits and losses

4. Account Management Risks

When you entrust your account to JMJ Trading for management:

  • You retain ownership of your funds, but we execute trades on your behalf
  • Our trading strategies may result in losses
  • Performance fees are deducted from profits only, but losses are solely yours
  • Past performance of managed accounts does not guarantee future performance

5. Pool Funding Risks

Pool funding involves combining capital with other traders:

  • Your funds are pooled with others, reducing individual control
  • You share in both profits AND losses proportionally
  • Pool performance depends on the collective trading strategy
  • Withdrawals may be subject to liquidity windows

6. Signal Service Risks

Following trading signals involves specific risks:

  • Signals are provided for informational purposes only
  • You are solely responsible for your trading decisions
  • Technical issues, internet connectivity, or platform delays may affect execution
  • Slippage may occur between signal publication and your trade execution
  • Your broker's execution speed and policies affect results

Our mentorship program provides education and guidance, but:

  • Learning does not guarantee profitable trading
  • You are responsible for applying what you learn
  • Past performance of mentors does not predict your future results
  • Individual results vary based on discipline, risk management, and market conditions

8. Broker Risks

We recommend and work with Deriv broker, but:

  • We are not affiliated with Deriv beyond our referral relationship
  • You are responsible for choosing your broker and understanding their terms
  • Broker insolvency, technical issues, or policy changes may affect your funds
  • Always verify your broker's regulatory status and security measures

9. Technical Risks

Technical failures can impact your trading:

  • Internet outages, power failures, or device malfunctions
  • Platform downtime or delays in signal delivery
  • Third-party service disruptions (Telegram, email, etc.)
  • Cybersecurity threats including hacking or phishing

10. Psychological Risks

Trading psychology significantly impacts results:

  • Emotional decision-making (fear, greed, revenge trading)
  • Overtrading after losses or wins
  • Failure to follow risk management rules
  • Stress affecting personal and professional life

11. Only Trade with Risk Capital

NEVER trade with money you cannot afford to lose. This includes:

  • Rent or mortgage payments
  • Emergency savings
  • Education funds
  • Retirement savings
  • Borrowed money
  • Living expenses

12. Know Your Risk Tolerance

Before using our services, honestly assess:

  • Your financial situation and ability to withstand losses
  • Your investment objectives (short-term vs long-term)
  • Your experience level with trading and financial markets
  • Your emotional capacity to handle market volatility
